Category icon options · Kitchen, Pets, Bugs

Each icon switches its tile to match the background: on dark colors it uses the Glow tile (white icon on a deep VYBEON gradient), and on light colors it uses the Light chip (navy icon on white with a gradient ring). The two large tiles show both versions. The small strips show what you'd actually see on the VYBEON navy and on every color of all 7 swatch themes. Pick one per category, for example K2, P1, B2.

Contrast: the icon on its tile is 5.8:1 (glow) and 18.1:1 (light). Every tile has a navy outer ring and a white inner ring, so its edge stays at least 4.4:1 against all 38 background colors. The standard for icons is 3:1.
